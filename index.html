How it works

A guide, not a final result

Each assessment is converted to its QCAA contribution: your mark ÷ task maximum × assessment weighting. Your projection is contributions earned ÷ weightings entered × 100. That percentage is compared with the 2025 grade boundaries for your subject. If you enter only one IA, it assumes you keep performing at the same rate on every remaining assessment.

Mathematics and Sciences have 50 marks of IAs and a 50-mark EA. The other subjects shown have 75 marks of IAs and a 25-mark EA. If your school task is marked out of a different total, change its “Marked out of” field; for example, 24/30 on a 10% data test contributes 8/10 to the final subject mark.

Actual IA results may change through confirmation; future tasks and the EA may differ from your current results. QCAA sets the grade boundaries for each cohort, so the 2025 boundaries are a reference rather than a promise of your final grade. This calculator covers the listed General subjects; Applied and Essential subjects use different reporting and are not included.
